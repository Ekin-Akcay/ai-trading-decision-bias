# Descriptive AI behavior review

This review will run only after the root agent explicitly releases completed confirmation results. It makes no new trade-model calls, scores no unused reserve windows, changes no strategy, and supplies no additional hypothesis test or candidate search. Screen and confirmation stay separate.

Use only audited stage trade ledgers and their saved 120-minute native paths, plus original snapshot MA20/MA50 metadata and accepted rationale/confidence. Report the number of opinions and executed policy trades for every group. AI confidence is the model's self-reported field, not an independently calibrated probability of success.

1. **Moving-average agreement.** Compare each original AI action with the simple direction implied by the original price versus MA20: long above/equal, short below. Repeat descriptively for MA50. Show the cross-tabulation of actual long/short calls and above/below sides; separately show both MAs below price, both above, and mixed. A correlation does not establish that the MAs caused a decision. Report these counts by stage, gate direction, and asset; do not fit another classifier.
2. **Confidence.** Show sample size, mean, median, interquartile range, and range for all calls, selected/opposed calls, and winning/losing selected positions. Do not create a confidence cutoff or trade policy.
3. **Written rationale.** Retain verbatim reason text. Count overlapping, fixed literal keyword categories: moving averages (`moving average`, `ma20`, `ma50`, `20ma`, `50ma`, `20-period`, `50-period`); continuation/trend (`trend`, `continuation`, `lower high`, `lower low`, `higher high`, `higher low`); exhaustion/reversal (`exhaust`, `reversal`, `overextend`, `overbought`, `oversold`, `rebound`); breakout/rejection (`breakout`, `breakdown`, `reject`, `failed`, `failure`); levels (`support`, `resistance`, `range`, `swing`); volume (`volume`). Matching is case-insensitive substring matching and categories can overlap. Counts describe wording, not proof of causal reasoning or calibrated market insight. No word pattern becomes a filter.
4. **Position paths.** For selected winning and losing trades, report entry-relative signed returns at 15, 30, 60 and 120 minutes, MFE, MAE, and first minute of maximum favorable/adverse excursion. Show medians and interquartile ranges with explicit group sizes. These groups are defined after the outcome and are descriptive, not signals available at entry. Intrabar high/low order is unknown. We will not simulate alternate exits or stops in this review.
5. **Concrete examples.** From valid AI-selected confirmation positions only, choose the highest return, the lowest return, and the lower-median return. Sort returns ascending, then case ID ascending to break ties; lower-median index is `(n-1)//2`. If an example duplicates another, include it once. Quote its original reason, confidence, MA side, pre-entry gate move/volume, delayed entry/exit, and four signed path checkpoints, with MFE/MAE. These declared ranks avoid handpicking an attractive story; the extremes are explicitly exceptional.

The final `AI_BEHAVIOR.md` will distinguish descriptive model behavior from the frozen confirmation verdict. It will not pool screen and confirmation to rescue a failed rule, claim market accuracy from MA agreement, or infer a retail-trader mechanism from language alone.
