## Question

Can the model's directional errors be exploited after realistic fixed costs, with profitable payoff geometry and an advantage over a price-only rule? A majority win rate is insufficient: the latest short rule averaged +0.764% on winners and −1.498% on losers. At that payoff mix, gross break-even win rate is about 66.2%, versus 57.1% observed; with 20 bps cost, break-even is about 75.1%. A smaller stop changes both payoff and win rate, so it must be simulated on the actual paths.

Root also examines budget/sample feasibility without viewing fresh outcomes.

## Validation boundary

Before fresh model calls or forward-price evaluation, freeze final candidate definitions, sampling rules, accounting, statistical tests and success criteria in a separate immutable protocol. No more than three primary final candidates. One chosen model (Terra) for continuity; no model switching based on new outcomes. New windows are historical, previously unused and separated from all existing experimental windows. They are not future-live or necessarily unseen in model training. Shared windows and changing market regimes must not be counted as independent model opinions.

Use a fixed validation sample size and a preserved seed; do not add more windows because a p-value is nearly significant or change rules after seeing validation returns. Report costs, slippage/latency sensitivity, conservative intrabar ambiguity, time-period stability, tail concentration and simple price-only controls. Positive net average alone is insufficient to call a strategy verified. Fresh evidence must support both positive expectancy and useful AI contribution with multiplicity handled across frozen finalists.
