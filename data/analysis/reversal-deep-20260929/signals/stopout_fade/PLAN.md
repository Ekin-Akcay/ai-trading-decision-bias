# Stop-out flow fade:16-cell exploratory mechanism

Frozen before computing this mechanism's outcomes on2026-09-29, after inspection of the earlier primary tests and entry-grid results. This entire study is exploratory reuse of the original150 and subsequent280 Terra windows. It is not untouched validation. No model calls or new price-regime gates.

Hypothesis: a retail-like AI may get timing/stop placement wrong. Its forced stop sale could be followed by recovery. This strategy therefore **follows the original AI direction after its stop-out**, which fades the direction of the forced closing flow; it does not reverse the AI's initial entry direction.

Finite16 policies: original/strategy side LONG or SHORT × maximum original-bracket waiting time105or240minutes × holding time60or240minutes after actual new entry × new exit policy time-only or ATR1stop/ATR2target with time cap. No further cells, filters or thresholds after results.

For a LONG policy, the hypothetical original trade starts at the original visible close, stop below entry by the AI's supplied stop fraction, target above by exactly2×that fraction. For SHORT, signs reverse. Require that the original bracket hits SL FIRST within the waiting limit. If its target wins first, no trade: it is no longer a forced stop-out. Opening gaps are checked before within-minute extremes; adverse gaps count as SL, favorable target gaps count as TP, and a normal bar touching both levels is conservatively SL first. This exact interpretation was agreed with root before scoring; there is no alternative later-SL-barrier variant.

If the original bracket's first exit is SL, enter a new position in the SAME original side at the following1-minute bar's open, after the entire trigger bar has completed. New position follows the AI direction and opposes the forced closing flow. If the first future bar(t0to t0+1minute) triggers, new entry is t0+1minute; if the last105-minute waiting bar triggers, entry is t0+105minutes. Time-only exit is the close exactly60/240minutes after new entry. The ATR bracket uses the pre-original-entry ATR14 percentage, stop1×ATR and target2×ATR recentered on actual new-entry price, with60/240-minute cap. Gap stops fill at actual adverse open, gap targets are capped at target, both-level ambiguity counts as stop. Include the entire new-entry minute, since entry is at its open. The maximum elapsed path is480minutes, below the available24-hour horizon.

Primary selected mask requires actual AI action to match the policy side. Two controls: (1) identical hypothetical original stop/target and ensuing strategy on every window using that window's own AI-supplied width regardless of AI action; label **AI-width control, not AI-free**; (2) original first-exit bracket with stop1×pre-entry ATR and target2×ATR, on every window, then the identical same-side following trade. Label this price-only ATR-width control; it changes trigger geometry and potentially entry timing, so it does not isolate AI direction alone. All use identical side, waiting/holding caps, exit convention and20bps roundtrip for the new trade only. The hypothetical original AI loss is not booked as strategy P/L.30bps is a fixed sensitivity.
