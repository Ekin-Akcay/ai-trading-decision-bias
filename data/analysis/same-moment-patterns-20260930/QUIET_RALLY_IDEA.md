# Quiet-rally short: retained research hypothesis

Wait 30 minutes after an existing AI snapshot. If price has risen at least half of the original ATR14 and those 30 minutes have at most 75% of the median 30-minute volume from the preceding 50 hours, short at the next minute open. Exit after 120 complete minutes. No AI-direction filter, stop or target.

Observed: 56 positions /46 event clusters, +0.3921% mean gross, 60.7% wins; removing the best three event clusters leaves +0.1038%. The rule survives fixed 1/5-minute entry delays descriptively. Whole-search uncertainty includes losses. This needs separate verification before a profitability claim; no additional calls are queued.

The rule is price-only. An after-the-fact subset with original AI SHORT has a larger mean, suggesting delayed entry in the original direction, but no AI-specific effect is established. Full results, failures and source cohorts are in RESULTS.md.
