# Pre-scoring descriptive leg decomposition

Root requested this addition after the complete sample/features/predictions were frozen and before any new returns were computed or examined.

Alongside the unchanged primary two-sided equal-quarter basket, report the proxy's selected LONG and SHORT counts, gross mean per leg trade, summed-return contributions, and each leg's contribution per all sampled asset opportunities / four-asset basket. Include year and asset breakdowns so the user's preference for longs can be understood descriptively.

This adds no separate trading rule, side selection, threshold, fit, horizon, subgroup profit test or opportunity to promote a side. Neither a favorable long nor short component may rescue a failed pooled primary or establish an additional significance claim. The frozen sample, primary policy, MA20 comparator and two primary tests remain unchanged.
