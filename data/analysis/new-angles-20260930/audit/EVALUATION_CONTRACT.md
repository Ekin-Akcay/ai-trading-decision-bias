A positive policy mean versus cash is insufficient for an AI mechanism. Show a paired opportunity-return difference against the simplest price-only policy with matching tradable clocks, risk/market exposure and eligibility. Preserve zeros for skips, report conditional executed-trade means alongside opportunity means, and report complementary AI groups. A control that retains the current AI stop width, AI confidence, reason tag or model-derived donor width is AI-derived, even when it drops action. Explicitly label it and add a price-only geometry/control.

For AI-surprise studies, compare the full strategy with exactly the same forward-trained price model and its predicted action/confidence, and with raw AI direction. Lexical tags and confidence have already been tested; new residualization is the substantive extension, not a new name for their unconditioned correlation. For target/stop-reaction studies, compare the same event state machine with pre-entry price-derived levels (ATR or visible structure), plus frozen width/placebo controls if used. ## Statistical and economic credibility

Use calendar blocks for within-study resampling; synchronized assets and repeated calls stay together. Report whole-quarter resampling as a dependence/regime sensitivity, chronological halves/thirds, every cohort, and per-asset results where relevant. An AI-direction residual can add forecast accuracy without profitable payoffs; conversely price drift can produce profit without AI value. Evaluate both and name them separately.

Define the entire new finite family before computing its joint diagnostic. For fixed per-block policies, use shared resamples and a max-statistic or analogous complete-family correction on all candidate profit vectors; analyze AI-minus-control vectors as a second explicitly declared family. Include sign flips, alternative response features, horizons and gates as separate searched definitions. For learned pipelines, a frozen-policy payoff bootstrap is conditional on fitted models; do not claim it incorporates training uncertainty. A nested refit with forward folds is needed if formal pipeline inference is attempted. FDR may describe many associations; it does not by itself verify a selected trading strategy.

Retain prior11295-definition and later-study search history. A new-family adjusted p-value is a retrospective diagnostic for that family, not global immunity from all past selection. Do not reset multiplicity by moving files or calling a rule novel. No result in this phase may be labelled fresh confirmation, independent validation, reliably profitable or proven retail behavior.

Report mean, median, win/payoff sizes, opportunities/executions, worst loss, maximum excursions, arithmetic gross notional return and exact assumptions. Include removal of largest1and3 positive calendar contributions, positive/negative contribution shares, leave-one-quarter-out means, and fixed1/5-minute entry latency sensitivities where executable. Do not turn removal of a realized loser/winner into a trading filter. For a multi-leg portfolio, use complete basket returns for concentration, not individual-leg pseudo-replication.

## Gate before even proposing any further model calls

All gates must be answered explicitly in CANDIDATE_GATE.json; nothing here authorizes spending.

1. **Distinct mechanism:** name the closest earlier study and show a materially different pre-entry information channel, ordered reaction event, or synchronized relative-value construction. Fee/exit/threshold retuning alone fails this gate.
2. **Executable:** independently reproduce selected and skipped windows, known-at-entry features, native-minute fills and all exposure/accounting. Use one fixed direction/execution definition; no post-result interpretation switch.
3. **AI increment:** positive paired opportunity advantage over an adequate price-only control, not only an AI-width control. Sufficient within-context contrasts must exist; all-selected strata cannot identify AI contribution. Require the sign to survive the declared calendar/quarter sensitivities, and show every comparator.
4. **Support:** aim for at least50 active non-overlapping calendar blocks, at least20 executed blocks in each chronological half, and at least8 populated quarters before describing a candidate as ready for a small replication. These are practical screening floors, not sufficient statistical power. Smaller subsets remain descriptive/mechanism pilots; compute the actual precision rather than treating high win rate as evidence. If an originally small90-block disagreement sample cannot meet this screen, do not manufacture count by treating arms or assets independently.
5. **Stability and search:** positive gross opportunity and incremental point estimates in both chronological halves, positive mean after removing the best3 calendar contributions, no dependence on one source cohort, and a favourable complete-new-family diagnostic. Report all failed checks. Strong ordinary p-values after an adaptive search are insufficient. A small positive mean with a wide interval does not justify another wave by itself.
6. **Useful prospective measurement:** freeze the exact hypothesis, model/input intervention and minimum economically meaningful effect; estimate eligible trigger rate and sampling precision. State what new information the proposed calls can distinguish from a simpler price-only experiment or a data-only replay. Count new calendar observations, not opinions. The29 remaining calls generally cannot settle a modest noisy return edge, especially after filtering; they might only measure a deliberately narrow behavioural mechanism. No optional extension until a p-value crosses a line.
7. **Separate decision:** root reviews the complete artifact and user's current budget direction before any future collection. A favourable exploratory candidate gate is a reason to discuss a concrete prospective design, not automatic authorization to spend. Current instruction remains ZERO calls.

Failure of one proposed mechanism is information, not permission to rename another old rule or consume the remaining budget. The scope here is to find genuinely new, testable structure in existing opinions and paths while being honest about what those data can establish.
