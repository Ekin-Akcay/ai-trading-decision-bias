## Two co-primary rules

**L048: long against bearish AI in a daily downtrend.** The asset's previous completed UTC daily close is at or below its 50-day simple moving average, and Bitcoin's previous completed UTC daily close is at or below its 200-day simple moving average. If Terra chooses SHORT, enter LONG at the first minute open immediately after the visible chart ends. Exit at the close 240 minutes after this scheduled entry. No stop/target, confirmation, confidence or other filter. For BTC, the two conditions apply to BTC's 50- and 200-day averages. This translates an existing SOL discovery rule to four assets; cross-asset transport is being tested, not assumed proven.

**S508: short against bullish AI above both intraday averages.** Current normalized price 100 is at or above both MA20 and MA50. If Terra chooses LONG, enter SHORT at the last visible close. Stop distance is two times the pre-entry ATR14 percentage, target distance equals the stop distance, timeout 240 minutes. This is discovery policy508 with the above-both gate. Fixed percentage rule179 is not a primary candidate.

S uses actual native minute OHLC. Adverse gaps fill at open, favorable target gaps fill at target; if both stop and target touch inside one minute with neither crossed at open, stop wins. No restoration after a stop. L uses actual open/close prices. Primary cost is exactly zero: gross percentage equals direction sign times (exit/entry minus one) times 100. No funding, commissions, spread or leverage in primary returns, per user instruction. This exclusion is an assumption, not evidence that leverage eliminates costs.

For both rules, prespecified 1- and 5-minute entry delays preserve the original 240-minute exit clock; S recenters its bracket on actual delayed entry.
